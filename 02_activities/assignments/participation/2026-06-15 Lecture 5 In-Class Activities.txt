Q. How did this diagram of the Brooks use rational, moral and emotional appeal to make a case to audiences?

rational appeal - shows this though very specific numbers to show actual conditions within the ship (e.g., 130 slaves being stowed under shelves)
moral appeal - schamatic shows people having to crouch to move around the ship, showing just how cramped people are shows it may be immoral to have so many people on each ship
emotional appeal - evokes feelings of injustice by depicting the really hard conditions on the ship (e.g., lack of personal space, probably hygiene and safety concerns)
